## Delivery and commissioning

- Measured and labeled route obstacles, checking an approximately **90 in transport height against 92 in minimum clearance**.
- Coordinated riggers, hatch protection, equipment relocation and delivery access. The machine arrived on **July 30, 2026**.
- Led Facilities preparation for HVAC readiness, temporary door removal and electrical shutdowns.
- Coordinated the **30 A to 50 A power upgrade**, including transformer delivery, wiring, disconnect and fuses, and worked with Facilities, electricians and DMG MORI on commissioning and phase rotation checks.

## Compressed-air system

- Designed filtration, oil removal, desiccant drying and pressure regulation.
- Specified a **15 SCFM dryer**, afterfilter and shutoff/vent valve for servicing.
- Designed strut and pegboard mounting with brackets, spacers and dryer supports.
- Specified **3/8-in connections**, hoses and machine couplings, and coordinated air and argon parts procurement.
